# Recorder guide

**Sign in** at the app address with the email and password you set from your invitation.

**Add an entry** (New entry):
1. Pick *Expense* or *Income*, the date, the category (Fuel, Fooding, Staff…) and how it was paid.
2. Enter the amount, who it was paid to (or received from) and a short note saying what it was for.
3. Attach the bill or voucher: take a photo or choose a file. Photos are shrunk automatically to under 200 KB. PDFs must already be under 200 KB. If the photo is very small (under 50 KB) the app asks you to confirm it is readable.
4. Save. The entry becomes **Pending** and goes to the admin.

**After saving**: *Pending* entries can be edited. *Approved* and *Exported* entries are locked. *Rejected* entries show the admin’s note: fix the problem and save again to send it back for review.

Tips: the form remembers an unfinished entry if you lose signal. Never enter the same bill twice; the app warns you if it looks like a duplicate.
